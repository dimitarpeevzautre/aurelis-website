ABOUT AURELIS

We didn't convert a van. We waited for the right one.

Aurelis started in 2023 in a workshop outside Rotterdam, with a conviction and a waiting list of one. The conviction: the pop-top camper — perfected over sixty years on combustion vans — was about to be reinvented by the electric platform, and nobody was building for it properly. Retrofitting diesel-era furniture into an EV wastes everything the platform offers.

So we waited for the right base vehicle. The Kia PV5 Passenger gave us what no combustion van could: a completely flat floor, factory side glazing and homologated seating to build around, a 71.2 kWh battery that doubles as camp infrastructure, vehicle-to-load power built in, and a 7-year factory warranty that survives conversion.

Every Aurelis One is converted in-house — furniture CNC-cut from poplar ply and recycled aluminium, wired into the vehicle's own high-voltage system in partnership with Kia-certified engineers. We build slowly, in the order interest was registered, and we'd rather cap production than compromise the build.

What we hold ourselves to

01

Electric-native, not retrofitted

Every system — heating, cooling, cooking, lighting — is designed around the high-voltage platform. No gas, no diesel heater, no generator, ever.

02

The warranty survives

Our conversion is engineered and certified so the Kia 7-year/150,000 km factory warranty remains fully intact. Your camper is serviceable at any Kia dealer.

03

Built to be repaired

Modular furniture on a rail floor, standard fasteners, published part numbers. Any module can be removed, repaired or upgraded — including by you.
