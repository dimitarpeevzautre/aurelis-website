WHY AN ELECTRIC FLEET

Lower running costs. Cleaner cities. Happier renters.

An electric fleet runs for a fraction of diesel per kilometre, gets into every low- and zero-emission zone without a surcharge, and stands out on the forecourt. The 71.2 kWh battery doubles as camp power — a feature renters actively choose — and the Kia 7-year/150,000 km warranty follows every conversion, so your assets stay serviceable and resellable.
